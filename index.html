Our story

Made by hand, named with care

BeadRev began as a way to give jewellery a deeper story — every piece carries a Yorùbá word and the feeling behind it: success, joy, favour, faith, love, protection.

Each bracelet and necklace is strung by hand, in small batches, here in Nigeria — so what you wear feels considered, not mass-made.

Good to know

Handmade with care

Keep your piece away from water, perfume and lotion. Make it the last thing you put on and the first thing you take off, and store it in its soft pouch when you're not wearing it.

For sizing, measure your wrist and add about 2cm — or message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you. Custom sizes are always welcome.
